## Definition

A promotion has one job: change behaviour you would not otherwise get. If the
customer was going to buy it anyway, you ran a discount, not a promotion.

## Industry best practices

- **Pick one goal per promotion.** Clear cash, build baskets, or bring back
  lapsed customers. A promotion aimed at all three achieves none.
- **Anchor a slow product to a fast one.** "Buy any two spirits, get the
  Prosecco at $14.99" moves the Prosecco using demand that already exists.
- **Two weeks maximum.** Longer and it becomes the price.
- **Give staff one line.** The register is where a promotion is made or lost.
  If your staff cannot say it in a sentence, customers will not hear it.
- **Measure against the weeks before**, not against your hopes.

## Common mistakes

- **Promoting what is already selling.** Margin donated.
- **Promoting something out of stock.** Demand you cannot serve, sent next door.
- **Too many products.** A campaign naming sixteen products names none.
  Three to five is a campaign; sixteen is a catalogue.
- **No physical presence.** An SMS with nothing on the shelf converts poorly.
- **Running the same offer continuously.** Customers learn to wait for it.

## Recommended actions

1. Choose the **goal** first, then the products that serve it.
2. Pick **three to five products**, at least one a proven mover as anchor.
3. Set a **two-week window** with a hard end date.
4. Put it on the **shelf, the door and one channel** (SMS or email — the one
   with more opted-in customers).
5. Compare the two weeks after against the two weeks before.

## KPIs

- Revenue lift vs the prior equivalent window
- Units moved on the target products
- Basket size during the promotion
- Redemption rate if the offer is coded

## Benchmarks

- Direct SMS/email response: **5–10%** of those contacted
- Bundle attach rate: **10–15%** where the pairing is natural
- Promotion window: **2 weeks**

## Store tips

The most under-used promotion in an independent is the **staff pick**. It costs
nothing, needs no discount, and works because it is the one thing the shop has
that a chain does not: a person with an opinion.

## AI notes

Never recommend promoting a sold-out product. Never propose more than five
products. Always give the window and name the goal. Check customer segments
before recommending a message-based campaign — a segment with nobody in it makes
the campaign pointless.
