## Characteristics

The fastest-growing spirits category in the US for a decade, and the one where
premiumisation is most visible. Blanco is unaged — bright, for margaritas and
volume. Reposado rests 2–12 months in oak: rounder, the sipping and gifting
default. Añejo ages 1–3 years and behaves like whiskey commercially. Cristalino
is aged then filtered clear, and sells largely on the bottle. Mezcal is smoky,
made from any agave, and a different customer entirely.

## Buying behaviour

Occasion-driven and increasingly premium. Tequila buyers moved up-market faster
than any other category — a customer who bought $25 blanco three years ago is
buying $60 reposado now. Celebrity brands drive genuine traffic and should be
stocked even at thin margin, because they bring people through the door.

## Profit profile

Strong and improving. Premium tequila carries whiskey-like margins with faster
turns. Blanco at the volume end is price-shopped; reposado and above are not.

## Seasonality

Cinco de Mayo is the single biggest day. Summer overall is blanco and margarita
territory — Memorial Day through Labor Day. December shifts to añejo and
cristalino as gifting. Buy blanco deep in April, reposado deep in October.

## Display strategy

Group with mixers, limes and salt during summer — an occasion bay outsells the
same bottles filed alphabetically. Premium bottles are visually striking and
deserve eye level with space around them; tequila is bought with the eyes more
than any other spirit.

## Cross-selling and pairing

Margarita mix, triple sec, limes, salt, agave syrup, Mexican beer, glassware.
The margarita kit is the most reliable bundle in the summer shop.

## Premium upgrade path

Mixto → 100% agave blanco → reposado → añejo/cristalino. The strongest step is
mixto to 100% agave, because the reason is factual and easy to say across a
counter: what is actually in the bottle.

## Typical customer

Broad and younger than whiskey. Skews female more than other spirits. Highly
responsive to packaging and to recommendation.

## Suggested promotions

Margarita bundles in summer. Tasting flights across blanco/reposado/añejo
convert trade-ups exceptionally well. Cinco de Mayo needs stock in place by
mid-April — the buying window, not the holiday, is the deadline.

## Supplier notes

Agave pricing has been volatile; cost increases arrive with little notice.
Celebrity brands are heavily allocated and relationship-driven.

## AI notes

Blanco, reposado and añejo are not interchangeable in a recommendation — they
serve different occasions and different seasons. Summer recommendations should
favour blanco; December should favour reposado and añejo. Mezcal is a separate
customer, not a tequila substitute.
