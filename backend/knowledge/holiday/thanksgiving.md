## Products

Pinot Noir, Beaujolais Nouveau, Chardonnay, sparkling for the toast, cider, and
hosting spirits — bourbon and vodka. Two very different days, one week.

## Customer behaviour

**Thanksgiving Eve — "Blackout Wednesday" — is one of the busiest liquor nights
of the year.** Everyone is home, nobody is working Thursday, and the buying is
beer and party spirits. **Thanksgiving Day itself is wine**: bought for a table,
often by someone who does not usually buy wine and wants to be told what to get.

That second customer is the opportunity. They are anxious, unbriefed, and buying
for people they want to impress. A shelf talker saying "goes with turkey"
converts better than any discount.

## Recommended promotions

Mixed-case wine discount in the week before. A "Thanksgiving table" display with
three price tiers — $15, $25, $40 — so the customer chooses a band rather than a
bottle. Beer and party spirits front-of-store on the Tuesday and Wednesday.

## Bundles

- Table set: Pinot Noir + Chardonnay + sparkling
- Host gift: a good bottle with a gift bag, priced as one item
- Wednesday: beer case + whiskey

## Inventory planning and buying window

**Order 4–5 weeks out.** Beaujolais Nouveau releases the third Thursday of
November and is a hard date. Wine deliveries get tight in the second week of
November as everyone orders at once; ordering late means not getting the
allocation you assumed.

## Expected demand

The largest wine week of the year for most independents. Blackout Wednesday can
be the single highest-revenue evening of the year — staff it accordingly.

## Display and social

Wine display up two weeks ahead. Recipe and pairing cards next to the bottles.
Social the weekend before with pairing content, which performs unusually well in
this window. SMS Tuesday for Wednesday night.

## Supplier advice

Ask about Beaujolais allocation early. Gift packs for December arrive now — buy
them with December in mind, not November.

## Cross-selling

Corkscrews, decanters, glassware, cheese and charcuterie if carried. Gift bags at
the register.

## AI notes

Treat Blackout Wednesday and Thanksgiving Day as two different occasions with
different products. The wine customer here needs guidance more than discount, so
recommend shelf talkers and pairing displays over markdowns.
