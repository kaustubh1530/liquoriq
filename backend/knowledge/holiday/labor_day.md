## Products

Beer (cases and 12-packs, light lager and Mexican import), hard seltzer variety
packs, blanco tequila and margarita mix, rosé and crisp whites, RTD cocktails.
The through-line is portable, chilled and shareable.

## Customer behaviour

The last cookout of summer. Baskets are large, planned, and bought in the two
days before the weekend — Thursday and Friday are the whole event. Customers are
buying for a group, which means volume formats and multiple categories in one
trip. Price sensitivity is high on beer, low on everything else.

## Recommended promotions

Case discounts on beer and seltzer. A margarita bundle (blanco, mix, limes,
salt). "Cookout kit" cross-merchandising. Avoid discounting premium spirits —
this is a volume occasion, and the premium buyer is not shopping today.

## Bundles

- Margarita kit: blanco tequila + mix + limes + salt
- Cooler run: beer case + seltzer variety + ice
- Poolside: rosé + white + plastic stemware

## Inventory planning and buying window

**Order 3–4 weeks out. Have stock on the floor 10 days before.** Beer
distributors deliver frequently, so beer can be topped up late; tequila, rosé and
mixers cannot. The mistake that costs money is ordering the week of — the
delivery arrives Saturday.

## Expected demand

One of the top three beer weekends of the year alongside Super Bowl and July
4th. Seltzer over-indexes heavily. Expect a sharp drop the following week —
whatever is left is a next-May problem, so buy to sell through, not to be safe.

## Display and social

Entrance display by the Tuesday before. Red/white/blue if it suits the shop.
Social posts land best Wednesday and Thursday evening. SMS Friday morning to
opted-in customers catches the pre-weekend shop.

## Supplier advice

Ask for display support and free ice — both are commonly available and rarely
requested. Confirm delivery dates in writing; the weekend before Labor Day is
the busiest delivery window of the summer.

## Cross-selling

Ice, snacks, limes, cups, charcoal if you carry it. The Labor Day basket is the
most cross-sellable of the year.

## AI notes

Buying window is the deadline, not the date — recommendations after roughly 10
days out should shift from "order" to "promote what you have". This is a volume
occasion: recommend beer, seltzer and tequila, not premium whiskey. Rosé
recommended now is rosé that must clear now.
