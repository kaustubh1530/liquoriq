## Definition

The highest-response channel available to a neighbourhood liquor store, and the
easiest to destroy. An SMS list is built one register conversation at a time and
lost in a single bad week of over-sending.

## Industry best practices

- **Under 160 characters.** One message, one offer, one action.
- **Lead with the offer, not the greeting.** The preview line is what gets read.
- **Send Thursday or Friday late morning** for weekend trade. Never before 10am
  or after 8pm.
- **Twice a month maximum.** Weekly sending trains people to unsubscribe.
- **Segment.** A message to everyone is a message to nobody and it burns the
  list.
- **Include an opt-out and honour it instantly.** It is also the law.

## Common mistakes

- **Sending to everyone every time.** The opt-out rate is the real cost, and it
  is permanent.
- **Discount-only messaging.** Customers learn to wait for the text. Alternate:
  new arrival, restock alert, event, then an offer.
- **No shelf presence.** A text about a product the customer cannot find when
  they arrive converts once and never again.
- **Sending Monday morning.** Nobody buys liquor on Monday morning.

## Recommended actions

1. Pick **one segment** with a reason to hear from you.
2. Write **one sentence with a number in it** — a price, a percentage, or a date.
3. Send **Thursday or Friday, 10am–2pm**.
4. Make sure the product is **in stock and visible** before the send.
5. Measure the two weeks after against the two weeks before.

## Benchmarks

- Response: **5–10%** of those contacted
- Opt-out should stay under **1%** per campaign
- Frequency: **twice a month maximum**

## Store tips

The highest-converting SMS an independent can send is not an offer — it is a
**restock alert on an allocated bottle** to customers who asked for it. It feels
like a favour rather than an advert, and it is the reason people give you their
number in the first place.

## AI notes

Always check opted-in counts before recommending SMS — a campaign to an empty
list is not a campaign. Never recommend more than two sends a month. Response
rate is an industry assumption; segment size is measured.
